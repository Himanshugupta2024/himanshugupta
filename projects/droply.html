DROPLY

Local delivery, without the overhead of a big platform.

Overview

Droply is a local delivery platform concept designed to connect nearby senders and couriers directly, without the overhead of large logistics networks.

Problem

Small, local deliveries are often disproportionately expensive or slow when routed through large courier platforms built for scale rather than short-distance, on-demand drops.

Objective

Design a lightweight platform concept matching local delivery requests with nearby available couriers in real time.

My Role

Product and system concept design, covering the request-matching flow and core application structure.

Approach

The concept centers on a simple request → match → deliver flow, prioritizing proximity and availability over complex routing optimization.
