ADVANCED SMART SHOES

Footwear that senses, locates, and charges itself.

Overview

A wearable technology concept for smart shoes combining GPS location tracking with EAP-based (electroactive polymer) technology, aimed at self-sustaining, trackable footwear.

Problem

Wearable IoT devices are often limited by battery life and charging inconvenience, which reduces their practicality for continuous, everyday use.

Objective

Explore how footwear could integrate location tracking with an energy-harvesting or auto-charging mechanism to reduce dependency on manual charging.

My Role

Concept design covering the sensing/tracking module and exploring EAP-based energy capture integration.

Approach

The approach paired a low-power GPS/communication module with an energy-harvesting layer designed to capture mechanical energy from walking, feeding a small onboard battery.
