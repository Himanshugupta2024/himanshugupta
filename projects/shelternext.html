SHELTERLINK — IOT SMART DISASTER SHELTER

A shelter that senses conditions before people have to.

Overview

ShelterLink is a sensor-based monitoring system built for emergency shelters, tracking temperature, load, humidity, and occupancy, with wireless transmission and real-time disaster alerts. It's a published patent application (No. 202411050202).

The Problem

Temporary shelters set up during disasters often lack real-time visibility into conditions inside and around them — occupancy, humidity, structural load, or hazards — making coordination and safety harder for responders.

The Idea

Build a shelter monitoring system that tracks temperature, load, humidity, and occupancy, and transmits that data wirelessly to trigger real-time disaster alerts.

My Role

System design, sensor selection, and embedded architecture for the shelter's monitoring and alert layer.
