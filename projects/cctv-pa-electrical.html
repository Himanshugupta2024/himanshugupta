ElectricalSystems Design

CCTV / PA / ELECTRICAL DESIGN

Coverage, power, and cabling — planned before a single camera goes up.

Overview

System design and engineering documentation work for CCTV, public address (PA), and general electrical systems — planning coverage, cabling, and power before installation.

Problem

CCTV and PA installations without proper planning often end up with blind spots, weak audio coverage, or cabling that doesn't meet distance/power limits.

Objective

Produce clear layouts and documentation for camera placement, PA zoning, and the supporting electrical/cabling infrastructure.

My Role

Layout design for camera coverage and PA zoning, plus supporting electrical documentation for cabling and power routing.

Approach

Each layout starts from the site plan, positions cameras/speakers for coverage, and then works backward to cable routing and power/PoE requirements.

Technology

AutoCADCCTV Layout DesignPA System DesignElectrical Documentation

Architecture / System Design

See the Engineering in Practice → page for layout previews.

Challenges

Achieving full CCTV coverage without blind spots in complex multi-floor facility layouts; keeping cable runs within NEC-compliant maximum distances for PoE-powered cameras; managing the power budget for simultaneous camera, PA amplifier, and NVR loads; and coordinating speaker placement for uniform PA coverage without echo or dead zones.

Solutions

Used AutoCAD to map camera fields-of-view with overlap analysis ensuring zero blind spots. Designed structured cabling layouts keeping all PoE runs under 90m with patch panels at intermediate distribution frames. Created load schedules and single-line diagrams (SLDs) for proper electrical panel sizing. Optimized PA speaker placement using zone-based coverage calculations.

Results

Delivered complete CCTV, PA, and electrical design documentation packages compliant with NEC standards. Camera layout achieved 100% coverage across all designated areas. Electrical SLDs and load schedules approved by site engineers without revision. PA system zone design eliminated dead spots across the facility.

What I Learned

This work reinforced that good systems design is mostly about disciplined planning — the layout on paper determines how well the installed system performs.
