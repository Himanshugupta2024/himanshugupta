NIGHT VISION DRIVER ASSISTANCE

Seeing the road when the driver can't.

Overview

A vehicle safety concept aimed at improving night-driving visibility by detecting obstacles, pedestrians, or hazards beyond the range of standard headlights.

The Problem

Reduced visibility at night is a major contributor to road accidents, especially where pedestrians or animals appear outside the range of headlight illumination.

The Idea

Explore a driver-assistance concept that uses computer vision to detect obstacles in low-light conditions and alert the driver in time to react.

My Role

Concept design for the detection pipeline, exploring how infrared/low-light imaging could feed into a real-time object-detection model.
