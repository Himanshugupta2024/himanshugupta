AUTONOMOUS VEHICLE PROTOTYPE WITH OPTI-VISION

Teaching a small vehicle to see and decide for itself.

Overview

A Raspberry Pi-based embedded vision system ("Opti-Vision") built for an autonomous vehicle prototype, featuring lane tracking, signal recognition, and obstacle detection using OpenCV. Published patent application (No. 202411078533).

The Problem

Manually driven or remote-controlled vehicles depend entirely on an operator — there's no ability to perceive and react to the environment independently.

The Idea

Give a small vehicle platform a camera-based perception layer so it can track lanes, recognize signals, and detect obstacles without direct manual control.

My Role

Concept and system design for the vision pipeline (lane tracking, signal recognition, obstacle detection) and its integration with the vehicle's control logic.
