ROOM / PG / FLAT RENTAL PLATFORM

Finding a place to live shouldn't feel like a full-time job.

Overview

A property and rental-listing platform concept (previously explored as FlatFlick and ShelterLink) focused on making it easier to find and list rooms, PGs, and flats.

Problem

Renters searching for rooms or shared accommodation often rely on scattered listings, outdated information, and unverified contacts, making the search slow and unreliable.

Objective

Build a platform concept that centralizes listings, supports search/filtering, and streamlines the connection between renters and property owners.

My Role

Product concept and application design, covering listing structure, search/filter logic, and the core user flow.

Approach

The platform is structured around searchable listings with filters (location, budget, room type) and a direct contact flow between renter and lister.
