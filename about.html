I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineer who enjoys turning technical ideas into practical systems.

Outside of formal projects, I spend time on solar PV design work — AutoCAD drafting, electrical layouts, and system documentation — which keeps my engineering grounded in things that actually get built and installed. "Some ideas become projects. Some become patents. Some become lessons."
